PRIVACY · COOKIES

Privacy and cookie information.

This page explains the basic data flows on the portfolio website. It is informational and should be updated if the site's analytics, forms or third-party services change.

Current site model

The portfolio does not require an account or a website contact form. Project communication is initiated by the visitor through external links such as Telegram. Analytics may be enabled only after the site's consent flow, depending on the current production configuration.

01 / Data

What may be processed.

01

Technical request data

Hosting and infrastructure may process standard request information such as IP address, user agent and requested URL.

02

Analytics after consent

If analytics is enabled after consent, usage events may be processed by the configured analytics provider.

03

External contact

When you open Telegram, GitHub or another external service, that service processes data under its own terms and privacy policy.

04

No account on this site

The portfolio itself does not require registration or a persistent user profile.

02 / Choices

Cookie and analytics controls.

If a consent banner is shown, you can use it to control optional analytics. Browser settings may also be used to clear or restrict cookies.

Privacy question? Contact directly.

For questions about this website and its data handling, use the same direct Telegram contact.
