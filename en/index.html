FAQ

Before your
first message.

Can I start with a small task?+

Yes. A form, bug fix, integration or new feature can be a standalone task. Describe the outcome and share a project link.

Do you work with existing code?+

Yes. For an estimate, share the code or describe the issue, the current stack and the expected result. A full rebuild is not always necessary.

How do I get a price and timeline?+

Send the task, a link or a design. Price and timing depend on scope, integrations and the state of the project. We agree on the estimate before work starts.

What do the demos show?+

Product interfaces and workflows. Demo data is sample data, not client performance. Case studies describe the tasks and implementation.
